## Bio

Tomás Hernando Kofman is the founder of Not Diamond, the world's most powerful intelligent model router for coding agents. Not Diamond helps engineering teams achieve frontier quality at a fraction of the price by identifying when a prompt requires a more powerful model and when a cheaper one is sufficient. They work with some of the highest volume AI startups and enterprises in the world, including OpenRouter and SAP, and are backed by Jeff Dea, Julien Chaumond, Ion Stoica, and many others.

## Recurring Themes

- **Inference spend as an organisational crisis** — frames the cost problem with concrete data (Uber, Microsoft, Meta, Ramp AI Index, $500M CFO anecdote) rather than abstractions
- **Model arbitrage** — cheap/OSS models are catching up to frontier quality; the gap is exploitable through intelligent routing
- **Routing is not a gateway** — consistent insistence on the distinction between access (gateway) and selection (router)
- **Cache-aware routing** — treats KV cache economics as a first-class input to routing decisions, not an afterthought
- **RL framing for agentic routing** — long-horizon, sparse-reward routing policy as the right model for coding agent sessions
- **Privacy via metadata** — routing on derived, schematized metadata rather than raw prompts as the enterprise pitch
