## Talks at AIE 2026

## Recurring Themes

- The four-axis tradeoff space of any LLM gateway: availability, latency, guardrails, cost — and the impossibility of maximising all four simultaneously.
- Operational realities that standard microservice patterns (retries, circuit breakers) miss when applied to LLM workloads.
- Guardrails as a dependency with their own failure modes: the fail-open vs. fail-closed decision, time budgets, and placement strategies.
- Decentralised resilience vs. centralised governance as the correct architectural split for production gateway systems.
