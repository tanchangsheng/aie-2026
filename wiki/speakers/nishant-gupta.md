# Nishant Gupta

**Role:** Staff Software Engineer & Tech Lead
**Company:** Meta (Superintelligence Labs)
**LinkedIn:** [linkedin.com/in/nishantgupta-ai](https://www.linkedin.com/in/nishantgupta-ai/)

## Background

Staff Software Engineer and Researcher at Meta Superintelligence Labs, specializing in large-scale distributed systems, AI infrastructure, and operational resilience. Builds agentic infrastructure enabling AI systems to operate reliably in production — covering evaluation, auditing, safety controls, feedback loops, and human oversight.

Previously led Meta's next-generation elastic compute infrastructure, managing ~30% of fleet capacity across tens of millions of servers in 20+ geo-distributed datacenters, delivering billions of dollars in infrastructure savings. Designed and deployed Dynamic Idle Resource Leasing, a production system that safely oversubscribes datacenter capacity while preserving strict reliability guarantees. Research publications with 90+ citations.
