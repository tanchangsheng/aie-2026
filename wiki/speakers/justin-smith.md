## Background

Justin Smith is a Founding Product Engineer at Resolve AI, a platform for AI agents that handle on-call, incident response, and daily operational tasks in production environments. As a founding engineer, he works across the product and technical surface of Resolve AI's agent platform — including the background agent architecture, enterprise platform integrations, and the eval harness that calibrates agent reasoning to mirror senior engineer investigations.

## Recurring Themes

- **Invisible toil**: operational work that accumulates silently with no trigger, no owner, no sprint slot — and how background agents address it
- **Production Context as the dominant cost**: navigating environment complexity >> executing the task; the knowledge layer is larger and faster-growing than the work itself
- **Always-on agents**: persistent, schedule/trigger-driven agents with durable state as the operational runtime primitive
- **Human as verifier**: the engineer's role in an agent-run production environment is to verify agent findings, not to initiate the work
