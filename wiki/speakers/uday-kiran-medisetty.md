## Bio

Distinguished Engineer at Uber leading engineering for Agentic AI experiences across first-party and third-party surfaces, and GenAI initiatives for engineering productivity (agentic coding, AI debugging, code reviews, large-scale refactoring). Co-leads the company-wide engineering community shaping Uber's architecture, culture, and standards. Over 11+ years at Uber, led the ground-up rebuild of the Fulfillment Platform (powering billions of daily Mobility/Delivery transactions) and the Earner multi-gig platform. Previously a Staff Engineer at VMware building next-generation API infrastructure. MS Computer Science, Georgia Tech; BS, BITS Pilani, India.
