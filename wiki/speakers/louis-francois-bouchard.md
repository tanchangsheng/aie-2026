## Background

Co-founder of Towards AI, where he builds and teaches a practical toolkit for shipping reliable LLM products. Co-authored *Building LLMs for Production* (2024), a hands-on guide to prompting, fine-tuning, RAG, and evaluation. Runs the "What's AI" YouTube channel and newsletter (70K+ subscribers). Former Mila PhD candidate (2022–2024). Partners with founders and organizations on AI strategy, training design, and production workflows.
