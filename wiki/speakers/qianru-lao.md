## Background

Qianru works on infrastructure for large-scale model serving at OpenAI. Previously contributed to the open-source Delta Lake project at Databricks, and worked on distributed storage systems at Alibaba Cloud and infrastructure tooling at Google. Holds degrees in Computational Science and Engineering from Harvard and Computer Science from Sun Yat-sen University.
