## Recurring Themes

Osmani's keynote synthesizes several concepts he has been developing publicly on his blog:

- **Harness engineering** — the system around the model (prompts, tools, state, constraints, feedback loops) is now the engineering surface. Ref: addyosmani.com/blog/agent-harness
- **Loop engineering** — shift from writing prompts to designing the systems that prompt agents. Ref: addyosmani.com/blog/loop-engineering
- **Human verdict as the scarce resource** — as generation becomes cheap, the bottleneck moves to accountable human judgment at the highest-leverage checkpoint
- **Taste as the decay-resistant differentiator** — in a world where anyone can generate anything, what you choose to make is the differentiator
- **Three failure modes** — cognitive debt, cognitive surrender, orchestration tax — named anti-patterns for engineers working with AI
- **The Agency Ladder** — a 7-level framework for how much of a problem an engineer owns (FLAG → EXECUTE → DIAGNOSE → PROPOSE → RECOMMEND → RESOLVE → DISCERN)
