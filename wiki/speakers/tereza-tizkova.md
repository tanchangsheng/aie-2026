## Recurring Themes

- **Software factory as a whole-cycle discipline** — spec, build, validate, deploy, learn. Not just code generation.
- **Three-property framework:** agnostic (model-routing + caching), autonomous (orchestrator + workers + validators), always-improving (agent-readiness, encoded preferences)
- **Environment as the bottleneck** — agent results degrade not because of weak models but because of poorly structured codebases (missing hooks, undocumented env vars, absent AGENTS.md)
- **Reliability over capability** — task length doubles every 7 months, but at only 50% reliability; the hard problem now is long-horizon reliability
- **Abstractions move up, not away** — the human-computers analogy: engineers don't get replaced, they move to deciding what to build rather than how
